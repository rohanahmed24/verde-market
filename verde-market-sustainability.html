How we work

Lower waste at every handoff.

From farm to front door, each decision is designed to protect food quality while reducing unnecessary materials and miles.

We prioritize practical improvements that can be measured, repeated, and strengthened with our grower community.

package_2

Zero-waste packaging

All Verde packaging is compostable or recyclable, and single-use plastics are removed wherever a dependable alternative exists.

compost

Regenerative farming

Farm partners are selected for their commitment to living soil, thoughtful water use, crop diversity, and long-term land health.

local_shipping

Carbon-conscious delivery

Neighborhood orders are grouped into smarter routes, with remaining delivery emissions balanced through certified forestry projects.

A circular habit

Designed to come back, break down, or disappear.

A useful sustainability system should feel easy in a real kitchen. Our packaging choices are planned around what happens after the box is opened.

  1. 01

    Return the crate

    Reusable delivery crates can be handed back on the next scheduled drop.

  2. 02

    Compost the liners

    Plant-fibre liners and paper cushioning can return safely to a home compost stream.

  3. 03

    Recycle the rest

    Clear material labels make the remaining card and glass simple to sort correctly.
